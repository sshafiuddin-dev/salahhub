Something isn’t right

What you seeWhat to do
No sound at the adhanAlerts start as Message only; choose a sound (chapter 5). Tap the screen once after opening the app. Check the volume and that the tablet is not muted.
Tap to enable sound keeps appearingThe browser needs one tap after the app opens. Tap the button and the alert plays.
The screen goes darkTap Keep screen on, and set a long screen timeout or Auto-Lock to Never (chapter 1).
The page turns amber or dark at nightThat is Brightness at night. Change it, or set it to Same as by day, in chapter 6, or tap Brightness for now.
Adhan times differ from my masjidCheck the method and Asr setting, then adjust single prayers by a few minutes (chapter 2).
Iqama times look oldLook at Last synced beside Sync now on the Today screen: if it stands out in colour, the masjid’s times could not be read and these are the last ones saved. Check the Wi-Fi, then tap Sync now. Away from ISCJ, set your own (chapter 4).
The timetable shows tomorrow’s timesThat is meant to happen, from the Isha iqama until midnight, so the first column says Tomorrow (chapter 3). The date at the top still shows today.
A time has default under itNo time came from the masjid or from you, so the app added your own “minutes after adhan” setting. Set that prayer’s real time to clear it (chapter 4).
Adhkar, Quran or Month have goneThey moved under More in the row along the bottom, so the prayer times stay uncluttered (chapter 3).
The Hijri date is a day outMove it a day in Settings › Location (chapter 2).
Quran or adhkar won’t playAdhkar recordings and surahs not saved yet need the internet. Check the Wi-Fi. Saved surahs play offline: see Listening offline.
My settings have goneSettings live in the browser on this tablet. Clearing the browser’s data, or opening the app in a different browser, starts afresh.

Questions

Does it work without the internet?

Yes, mostly. The clock keeps running with the last times it read, and the alerts and the adhan recordings still play: the app keeps all of them on your device. Surahs you have played or downloaded play too (see Listening offline). Weather, adhkar recordings and new surahs need the internet.

Do I need an account? Does it cost anything?

No account, no cost, no ads. It runs in the web browser.

What does it keep about me, and what does it send?

Your settings, favourites and playlists are saved only on this tablet, in its browser. There is no account. Prayer times are worked out on the tablet itself. The app sends out only:

  • your place’s latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo, for the weather (rounded to about 100 m if you use your location), and any city name you search for;
  • a request to this site’s own server for the ISCJ iqama times, with nothing about you in it;
  • requests to mp3quran.net and hisnmuslim.com when you play a recitation or a dua.

The full list is under Settings › About › What leaves this device.

Can I use it on my phone too?

Yes. It works the same on a phone. Settings on the phone and the tablet are kept separately.

Where do the times, recitations and duas come from?

Every source is listed under Settings › About, under Sources & credits.

Still stuck?

Send feedback from the app: tap Feedback at the bottom right, or on a phone go to Settings › About › Send feedback. You can also open the feedback form from here. Say what you expected and what you saw. The app adds a picture of the screen you were on and a few details, such as the app version and the look you use, and shows you all of it before you send. You can remove the picture, attach your own instead, or leave the details out. Add your email if you would like a reply.
