What you seeWhat to do
No sound at the adhanAlerts start as Message only; choose a sound (chapter 5). Tap the screen once after opening the app. Check the volume and that this device is not muted.

Adhan times differ from my masjidCheck the method and Asr setting, then adjust single prayers by a few minutes (chapter 2).
Iqama times look oldLook at Last synced beside Sync now on the Home screen: if it stands out in colour, the masjid’s times could not be read and these are the last ones saved. Check the Wi-Fi, then tap Sync now. Away from the masjids the app knows, set your own (chapter 4).
The timetable shows tomorrow’s timesThat is meant to happen, from the Isha iqama until midnight, so the first column says Tomorrow (chapter 3). The date at the top still shows today.

Questions

Does it work without the internet?

Yes, mostly. The clock keeps running with the last times it read, and the alerts and the adhan recordings still play: the app keeps all of them on your device. Surahs you have played or downloaded play too (see Listening offline). Weather, adhkar recordings and new surahs need the internet.

Do I need an account? Does it cost anything?

No account, no cost, no ads. It runs in the web browser.

What does it keep about me, and what does it send?

Your settings, favourites and playlists are saved only on this device, in its browser. There is no account. Prayer times are worked out on the device itself. The app sends out only:

  • your place’s latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo, for the weather (rounded to about 100 m if you use your location), and any city name you search for;
  • a request to this site’s own server for a nearby masjid’s iqama times, with nothing about you in it;
  • on a new device, or when you tap Use my approximate location, a request to this site’s own server for the town your internet connection is in, which it does not keep;
  • requests to mp3quran.net and hisnmuslim.com when you play a recitation or a dua.

The full list is under Settings › About › What leaves this device.

Can I use it on my phone too?

Yes. It works the same on a phone. Settings on each device are kept separately.

How do I use it with a TV remote?

Use the arrow buttons to move from one button to the next; the one you are on has an outline. Press OK to choose it, and Back to close what is open. To open Quran, go down to the bottom bar and across to Quran. Anything that does not fit in the bar is under More at its end. While a surah plays, press Back on any screen to jump to the player: you land on Pause, and going right reaches the other buttons and × to stop. Press Back again to return to where you were. If your remote has Play/Pause, Next or Previous buttons, they work the player from anywhere. A keyboard works the same way, with Esc for Back.

Where do the times, recitations and duas come from?

Every source is listed under Settings › About, under Sources & credits.

Still stuck?

Send feedback from the app: tap More, then Feedback, or go to Settings › About › Send feedback. You can also open the feedback form from here. Say what you expected and what you saw. The app adds a picture of the screen you were on and a few details, such as the app version and the look you use, and shows you all of it before you send. You can remove the picture, attach your own instead, or leave the details out. Add your email if you would like a reply.
