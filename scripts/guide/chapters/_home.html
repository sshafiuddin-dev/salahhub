IqamaHub guide

Adhan and iqama times for your home, on any screen.

IqamaHub turns a spare tablet, phone, computer or smart TV into a prayer hub for your home: a large clock with today’s adhan and iqama times, alerts, the weather, a daily dua, adhkar and Quran recitation. This guide shows you how to set it up and how to use every screen.

Good to know

  • It runs in the web browser. There is no account to make and nothing to buy.
  • Your settings stay on the device you set them on.
  • Within 10 miles of a masjid the app knows, iqama times come from that masjid’s own timetable. Anywhere else, you set your own.
