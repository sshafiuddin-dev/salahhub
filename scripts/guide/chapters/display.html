Choose how the clock looks, and how bright the screen is by day and at night. All of it is under Settings › Display.

Clock styles

Six ways to show the time and today’s prayers. Pick one under Clock style.

Masjid board: the time in large print beside the countdown, and the prayers as one timetable, like the board at the masjid. Chapter 3 explains each part. 24-hour dial: the whole day as a ring, with a countdown in the middle. Analog clock face: hands, with the coming prayers marked round the edge. Sun path: where the sun is between sunrise and Maghrib. Day timeline: the day as a list, with iqama times on the right. Bedside, low glare: amber on black, for a bedroom.

Look and brightness

The top of the Display page.
  1. Clock style: the six styles above.
  2. Look: Printed timetable (paper and ink), Handwritten (blue pen on a ruled sheet), or High contrast (black and white, easiest to read from afar).
  3. Brightness now: Bright, Soft, Dim or Darkest. The Bright button at the bottom right of every screen does the same.

Night, duas and full screen

Further down the Display page.
  1. Dim automatically: After Isha, After Maghrib, or Off. The screen goes bright again at sunrise.
  2. At night: how dim it goes: Soft, Dim or Darkest.
  3. Show duas and adhkar and Show transliteration turn the dua on the Today screen, and its English letters, on or off.
  4. Weather background shows today’s weather moving along the top of the page. Realistic sky (the usual choice) draws the real sky: the sun on its path from sunrise to Maghrib, clouds drifting with the wind, rain and snow as heavy as they really are, fog, a soft glow behind the clouds in a storm, and at night the stars and the moon at the phase of the Hijri date. From Maghrib to sunrise the page turns to its dark look so the times stay easy to read. Printed, moving draws the same weather in ink; Printed, still keeps it still; Off leaves plain paper. Older tablets, and devices set to reduce motion, get a lighter version by themselves. There is no sky in the Handwritten and High contrast looks.
  5. Full screen layout: Times only, Clock, prayers and dua, or Automatic, which shows times only on small screens.
Full screen on a 7-inch tablet, with the Automatic layout.

Good to know

  • Arabic text size (Small, Medium, Large) is at the bottom of the page, with Keep screen on.
  • A web page cannot turn down the tablet’s backlight. Dim and Darkest darken the page itself. For a darker room, lower the tablet’s own brightness too.
